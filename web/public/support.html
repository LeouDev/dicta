Support

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check your spam or junk folder and give it a few minutes. You can request a new email from the sign-in screen.

How do I change my name, username or photo?

Go to your profile, tap Edit profile, make your changes and tap Save.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap ⋯ on a quote or profile, or long-press a comment, and choose Report or Block. Blocked people can't see your quotes, follow you or comment on your posts. You can unblock them in Settings → Blocked accounts.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything you've posted, and it can't be undone.

Something else?

Open an issue at github.com/LeouDev/dicta/issues. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information.
